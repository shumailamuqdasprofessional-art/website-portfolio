Our Story · 우리 이야기

It started with trust.

Empress Beauty Box began in Canada with one simple belief: everyone deserves skincare they can trust.

Korean skincare is loved all over the world, but finding the real thing isn't always easy. So we created The Skin Edit, a carefully edited selection of 100% authentic K-beauty, brought from Korea, through Canada, to Pakistan.

For the first time, Pakistan can shop genuine Korean skincare with total confidence.
